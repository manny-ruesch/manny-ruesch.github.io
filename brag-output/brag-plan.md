# /brag Plan: Manny Ruesch Portfolio & AI Systems

## 1. Planning Rubric
1. **What is the product?**
   Manny Ruesch's engineering portfolio showcasing production AI pipelines, n8n automations, and autonomous multi-agent tools.
2. **What problem does it solve?**
   Eliminates manual, repetitive workflows for teams by deploying autonomous pipelines and AI systems that run 24/7 with zero bottlenecks.
3. **Who is it for?**
   Founders, engineering leads, and fast-growing teams needing high-leverage marketing automation, AI agent orchestration, and automated pipelines.
4. **What is the primary aesthetic?**
   Neo-Brutalist Red + Light Warm Gray (`#FF385C`, `#000000`, `#F0F0F0`), bold high-contrast borders (3px solid black), sharp hard drop shadows (`6px 6px 0px #000`), technical JetBrains Mono & Space Grotesk typography.
5. **What is the chosen tone?**
   `polished` with high-velocity tech energy. Confident, crisp, evidence-backed.
6. **What is the hook?**
   "What happens when you give small teams AI superpowers?"
7. **What are the real highlights?**
   - 32 active production workflows (n8n & Python)
   - ~720 monthly operations automated
   - ~350 hours saved per month
   - End-to-end autonomous multi-agent pipelines (Gumroad, Whop, Dev.to)
8. **What is the punchline/outro?**
   "Designed & shipped end-to-end. Systems that run on their own."
9. **What format and duration?**
   1920x1080 (landscape, 16:9), 18.0 seconds total.
